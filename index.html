Research I've worked on with teams.

Predicting Neighborhood Gentrification in the United States

Machine learning across 24 U.S. cities

With Launa Greer, Marc Richardson, and Manasi Phadnis, we built a model that scores the socioeconomic status of every census tract in 24 cities. It then predicts which neighborhoods are likely to rise in status over five years, flagging where gentrification risk is highest.

Read the paper View the code

Economic Assessment of CPEC: The Case of a Power Project

Social cost-benefit analysis of a coal plant in Pakistan

With Syed M. Hasan, Hamza Ali, and Suniya Raza, we weighed the environmental costs of the Sahiwal Coal Power Project against the energy shortfall it relieves over 30 years. The plant is a net benefit at the lower bound of the social cost of carbon and a net loss at the upper bound. Published in China's Belt and Road Initiative in a Global Context, Volume II (Palgrave Macmillan, 2020).
